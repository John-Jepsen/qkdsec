# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1** as its Code of
Conduct. The full text is published at:

  https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

A plain-text copy is also available at:

  https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/code_of_conduct.md

## Summary

We pledge to make participation in the `qkdsec` community a respectful and
harassment-free experience for everyone. We expect contributors to act in good
faith, to be welcoming to newcomers, and to focus discussion on the technical
substance of the work.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — the GitHub repository,
issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and any other channel used for
project communication — as well as when an individual is officially
representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Reports of unacceptable behavior may be sent privately to the project
maintainer at **jjepsen15@protonmail.com**. All reports will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly. The reporter's identity will be kept
confidential to the extent permitted by the circumstances.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying the standards of acceptable
behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to
any behavior they deem inappropriate. Enforcement guidelines follow the
[Contributor Covenant Enforcement Ladder](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
